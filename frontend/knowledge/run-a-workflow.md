# Run it

Choose **Run** and provide any per-run information needed. It will pause for approval before a step sends or writes to another app, unless you have turned approval off for that step, or if it needs help with access or permissions in a browser window. **Run history** shows the results of every run, and if applicable where it paused or stopped.

Keep Cryogram running and your computer awake. Closing the browser tab does not stop it; choose **Quit** at the bottom of the sidebar to close Cryogram.

A run also pauses when a needed value is empty or a send gets no confirmation. If you close a question, use the waiting banner to reopen it.

Before a send, choose **Approve & continue**, **Approve, don't ask again** or **Decline**. **Decline** stops the run and keeps it in **Run history**. **Approve, don't ask again** applies to later runs too; to restore asking, click the step on the canvas and tick **Require my approval before this runs**.
